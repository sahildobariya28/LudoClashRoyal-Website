Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ludo Clash Royal. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle your data when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. However, we may collect the following non-personal data:

  • Device Information: Model, OS version, device language (for analytics & crash reports)
  • Gameplay Activity: Number of games played, time spent, features used
  • Ad Performance: Impression count, click-through rate (via ad network SDKs)

3. Use of Information

We use collected data to:

  • Improve game performance and user experience
  • Analyze gameplay behavior to improve future updates
  • Serve relevant and non-intrusive ads
  • Detect and prevent crashes, bugs, or app misuse

4. Ads & Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services that may collect limited, anonymous data:

  • Google AdMob: For displaying ads within the app
  • Firebase Analytics: To understand app usage trends
  • Crashlytics: To detect and fix app crashes

These services may use identifiers like Advertising ID (AAID) to serve better content. No personal information is shared with these providers.

5. Children's Privacy

We are committed to protecting children's privacy. Our app is designed to be safe and friendly for users of all ages.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
  • The content is free from violence, inappropriate ads, or harmful links.
  • Parental guidance is still recommended for better digital habits.

6. Data Storage & Security

We do not store any user data on our own servers. All analytics and ads data are managed securely by trusted third-party providers.

  • Data is encrypted during transfer via secure protocols.
  • Only anonymized, non-identifiable data is used for app improvement.

7. User Control & Consent

  • You are not required to register or share personal data to use the app.
  • You can clear data anytime by uninstalling the app.
  • If any permissions are requested (e.g., storage), they are only used for gameplay and never shared externally.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in this document. Continued use of the app after updates implies your acceptance.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns about our Privacy Policy, feel free to contact us at:

Email: support@ludoclashroyal.com
